📥 Radarr Integration —

Connect up to 2 Radarr servers for sending missing universe items to download. Typically one for 1080p and one for 4K.

📺 Sonarr Integration —

Connect up to 2 Sonarr servers for sending missing TV shows to download. Typically one for 1080p and one for 4K.

Auto-send missing shows Automatically send all missing queue shows to Sonarr when the queue refreshes (off by default)

⬇️ SABnzbd Integration —

Connect up to 2 SABnzbd servers for real-time download progress. Replaces the stale progress from Radarr/Sonarr with live data from the download client.

🔄 Watchlist Sync

Bi-directional sync between Radarr/Sonarr and your Simkl/MDBList watchlist. Runs at startup, daily at 2:30 AM, and in the background when you open Airing Soon.

Arr → Watchlist Sync missing Radarr/Sonarr items (monitored, no file) to your watchlist so they appear in Airing Soon
Watchlist → Arr Auto-add watchlist items to Radarr/Sonarr if not already there (uses first configured server)
